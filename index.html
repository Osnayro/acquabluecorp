Standard Engineering Schemes

Common Treatment Configurations by Industry

An informative guide to recommended technological sequences and process trains tailored to specific raw water matrices, effluent types, and industrial quality standards.

01

Brackish & Seawater Desalination

High TDS Matrix
Raw Water Matrix & Challenge:

Seawater, high-salinity brackish wells, total dissolved solids (TDS) & scaling minerals.

Target Quality Standard:

Potable drinking water or low-salinity industrial process supply (>99% salt rejection).

Recommended Engineering Process Train:
  1. Deep-bed multimedia filtration + 5µm cartridge microfiltration
  2. Antiscalant chemical dosing & pH adjustment skid
  3. High-pressure Reverse Osmosis (RO) with Energy Recovery Device (ERD)
  4. Post-treatment re-mineralization & UV germicidal disinfection
02

Boiler Feed & Thermal Process Water

Thermal & Power
Raw Water Matrix & Challenge:

Well or municipal supply with hardness (Ca/Mg), reactive silica, and dissolved gases.

Target Quality Standard:

Zero-scale, non-corrosive ultra-pure water for high-pressure steam turbines & boilers.

Recommended Engineering Process Train:
  1. Sodium cation exchange resin water softeners
  2. Activated carbon filtration for free chlorine removal
  3. Two-pass Reverse Osmosis or Cation/Anion Demineralizers
  4. Deaeration towers + O2 scavenger chemical dosing
03

Pharma & Food/Beverage Purified Water

Sanitary Grade
Raw Water Matrix & Challenge:

Potable municipal water requiring pyrogen-free, micro-biologically sterile purity.

Target Quality Standard:

USP Purified Water & WFI standards with ultra-low conductivity & zero bacteria.

Recommended Engineering Process Train:
  1. Sanitary pre-filtration + Tri-clamp softening skid
  2. Double-pass RO in 316L electropolished stainless steel
  3. Continuous Electrodeionization (EDI) module
  4. 185nm/254nm UV TOC destruction + Ozone recirculation loop
04

Industrial Effluents & Zero Liquid Discharge

Wastewater & ZLD
Raw Water Matrix & Challenge:

High COD/BOD wastewater, heavy metals, oils, suspended solids, and chemical effluents.

Target Quality Standard:

Environmental discharge compliance or 95%+ water recovery for internal facility reuse.

Recommended Engineering Process Train:
  1. Coagulation/Flocculation + Dissolved Air Flotation (DAF)
  2. Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) or Advanced Oxidation (AOP)
  3. High-Recovery Effluent RO (HERO / High-pressure UF)
  4. Evaporators & Crystallizers for Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
